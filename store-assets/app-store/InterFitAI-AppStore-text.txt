===== Description (2489/4000 characters) =====
Your personal trainer, nutrition coach and food tracker in one app, powered by AI.

InterFitAI builds your workouts, sets your macros, plans your meals and logs your food, all around your body and your goal. No guesswork. Just a plan that works, and the tools to stick to it.

• Workouts built around you
• Macros made for your body
• Meal plans that hit your targets
• Snap a photo to log your food
• An AI coach that knows your plan
• Honest progress tracking

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WORKOUTS BUILT AROUND YOU
Tell InterFitAI your goal, level, schedule and equipment, and it builds a complete program:
• Proven splits: Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, Full Body, Bro Split, or let the AI choose
• Sets, reps and rest for every exercise, in the right order: heavy lifts first, finishers last
• Injuries or exercises to avoid? Just say so ("no deadlifts or squats") and your plan works around them
• Exercise demos, workout logging, volume tracking, personal records and streaks

MACROS MADE FOR YOUR BODY
Your daily calories, protein, carbs and fat, calculated from your body, activity and goal. Protein is based on your estimated lean mass, so your targets stay realistic at any size.

MEAL PLANS THAT HIT YOUR TARGETS
Full meal plans built to your calories and macros, for any eating style. Allergies and foods you dislike are always left out. Swap any meal and the rest of the day re-balances to stay on target. Includes a grocery list and saved meals.

FOOD LOGGING IN SECONDS
• Snap a photo of your plate or a nutrition label and see every ingredient
• Search everyday foods, packaged products and restaurant meals
• Restaurant and brand figures for your own country
• Type a whole meal ("3 eggs on 2 slices of rye") and get one accurate total
• Build-your-own orders and extras counted part by part

ASK INTERFITAI
Your coach, 24/7. Ask about training, nutrition, exercise swaps or your plan and get answers based on your own targets.

BODY ANALYSER
Compare before and after photos for an honest read on your progress: estimated body fat, visible changes and clear next steps.

TRACK EVERY WIN
Your weight trend and 7-day average, personal records, workout volume and streaks, all in one place.
